1 · Load your fog

Where's my Sync folder?

Fog of World keeps a folder named Sync full of small files. Point the folder picker at it.

Dropbox sync: Dropbox/Apps/Fog of World/Sync.
OneDrive sync: the Fog of World/Sync folder in your OneDrive.
Otherwise: in Fog of World open Settings → Data / Backup, export a backup, and use the Sync folder inside it.

On a phone? Folder-picking is unreliable, and files kept only in Google Drive / iCloud may fail to download when picked. Easiest: load your backup as a .zip with the “…or a .zip” button, no unzipping needed.

Getting that .zip onto a phone: the Drive / OneDrive apps won’t let you download a whole folder. Open drive.google.com or onedrive.live.com in your phone’s browser and switch it to desktop mode (Chrome: ⋮ → Desktop site; Safari: ᴀA → Request Desktop Website). Then long-press the Fog of World folder, choose Download, and the site zips it up for you. Load the file it gives you with the “…or a .zip” button.

Your fog is read on your device and is never uploaded.

Show
Visited shades the ground you've already defogged. Streets in fog highlights the streets and paths still covered by fog, so you can spot your best defogging opportunities. In Plan mode, paths that you can't take (e.g. pedestrian paths while planning car routes) are faded. Defog reads the street map for the area on screen as you pan, so it works when you're zoomed in (about an area of a town).
