Where's my Sync folder?

Fog of World keeps a folder named Sync full of small files. Point the picker at that folder.

Dropbox sync: Dropbox/Apps/Fog of World/Sync.
OneDrive sync: the Fog of World/Sync folder in your OneDrive.
Otherwise: in Fog of World open Settings → Data / Backup, export a backup, and use the Sync folder inside it.

Everything runs on your device — your data is never uploaded.
